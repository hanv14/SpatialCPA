| R12 | **Spatial autocorrelation collapses in the expression path on real data** — the generated field carries **15 %** of STARmap's per-gene Moran's I (median 0.0703 against 0.4635), and **22 %** even when placed at real positions. Not an inversion: the predicted values are squashed into a narrow band, so `paper_morans_pearson` is noise about zero and its negative sign is meaningless — the interpretable quantity is `morans_median_pred` vs `morans_median_gt`. **Length-scale calibration and the layout head are both ruled out by direct experiment** (T10 pilot §6.2, §6.3). Remaining candidates: the step budget, and `text_emb_mode="medcpt"` which is testable only on the campaign machine. **The campaign is on hold until this is attributed** — a 200+ hour run against a 15-22 % field would measure the defect at scale. **LOCALISED at the pilot**: the chain measures median Moran's I of **0.9714** at the GRF prior, **0.9015** after the flow and **0.8607** at the decoded `mu` — then **0.1297** at the sampled counts. Stages 1-3 lose 0.11 in total; the count draw loses **0.73 in one operation**. Real tissue retains **62 %** across the same latent->counts step (0.7449 -> 0.4635); the model retains **14 %**. The output is not globally over-dispersed (variance 0.57x real, zero fraction 0.0075 vs 0.0068) — the mean-variance **slope** is wrong, 2.120 against 1.738, a 22 % error against T06's < 0.15 criterion, i.e. `mu` is spatially smooth but too flat in amplitude for its structure to survive sampling. **The budget is also ruled out** (14.1 % -> 18.4 % at 2x steps). **Calibration measured and insufficient — this is a DESIGN finding, not a tuning one.** T09 §2's `calibrate_detection` fits in 7.4 s and does exactly its job: the mean-variance slope goes 2.121 -> **1.840** against the tissue's 1.738, a relative error of 22.0 % -> **5.9 %**, passing T06's < 15 % criterion. **Retention does not move: 14.4 % -> 14.6 %** against the tissue's 62.2 %. Since the draw is spatially uncorrelated by construction, `I(counts)/I(mu)` is the share of between-cell variance carried by the structured mean: **15.3 % for the model against 62.2 % for real tissue.** The decoder reproduces the *pattern* of between-cell variation almost perfectly (`mu` at 0.861, above the tissue's own latent at 0.745) at a fraction of its *amplitude*, and fills the gap with dispersion — and calibrating `theta` matches a marginal, which only reallocates variance inside the same decomposition. Not the prior, the flow, the budget, the layout, calibration, or `text_emb_mode`. **Owed to T06**: why does `mu` carry only 15 % of between-cell variance? Candidates — the size-factor path, `decoder_mu_link` (softplus compresses dynamic range at means in the thousands, a regime the fixture never tested), and a ZINB NLL that can be reduced by widening dispersion rather than sharpening `mu` (connects to R4 and T06's covariance loss). **Campaign remains on hold** |
